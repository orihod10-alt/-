# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Primary:** Israeli homeowners (25–65) who suddenly have a plumbing problem. They are stressed, often mid-crisis (burst pipe, blocked drain, water heater failure), and scanning several options on mobile. They trust by reputation, speed of response, and clarity of pricing. They make the decision in under 2 minutes.

**Secondary:** The plumber himself — managing incoming leads, possibly viewing a simple job-tracking or availability panel in the future.

## Product Purpose

A marketing website for a professional plumbing service in Israel. The site's one job is to convert a stressed visitor into a phone call or WhatsApp message. It does this by establishing trust immediately, showing real proof of work, and making contact frictionless. Every section exists to support that conversion — nothing decorates without earning its place.

## Brand Personality

**Confident, clean, dependable.**

Three words: Precise. Available. Trusted.

Emotional goal: the visitor should feel relief within the first scroll — "this is the professional I was looking for." Not awe, not excitement — relief and confidence.

Voice: Hebrew, direct, no jargon. Short sentences. No filler phrases. Numbers over adjectives ("תוך 45 דקות" not "מהיר").

## Anti-references

- **Yad2 / local classifieds:** cluttered multi-ad layouts, low-resolution stock images, competing phone numbers, visual noise. The visual opposite of trust.
- **Generic Israeli contractor sites:** navy + white, clipart wrench logo, Comic Sans energy, Times New Roman headers, same template used by 10,000 service businesses. Instantly forgettable and interchangeable.
- **Tech-startup overdesign:** dark hero with neon glow, excessive scroll animations, parallax on everything, feels wrong for a tradesperson context.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust at first glance.** Within 3 seconds a visitor must feel "this is a real professional I can trust with my home." The hero must communicate this before any scroll — through visual quality, tone, and a visible credibility signal (years, reviews, response time).

2. **One path, one action.** Every section leads toward contact (phone or WhatsApp). No competing CTAs, no rabbit holes. Navigation is minimal. The primary action is always visible.

3. **Premium without pretension.** The aesthetic is clean and considered, not luxury for its own sake. It signals "I take my work seriously and I'll treat your home the same way." A homeowner should feel they're getting quality without being overcharged for branding.

4. **Show, don't tell.** Real numbers (years of experience, jobs completed, response time), real photos of work, real client reviews. Specificity builds trust faster than superlatives.

5. **Speed signals safety.** Response time and availability are competitive advantages in emergency plumbing. Surface them prominently and honestly — a stressed client needs to know help is actually close.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Language:** Hebrew, RTL layout throughout
- **WCAG AA** compliance — sufficient color contrast, readable font sizes, keyboard-accessible interactive elements
- **Mobile-first:** the majority of users arrive via mobile, often mid-emergency. Touch targets, font sizes, and CTA placement optimized for one-handed use
- **Reduced motion:** respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — this is a service site, not an entertainment product
